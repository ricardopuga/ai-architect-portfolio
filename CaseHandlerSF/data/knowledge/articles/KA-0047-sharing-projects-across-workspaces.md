## Choose an approach

| Approach | Plans | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Invite people as Guests** | Team+ | A few people from another workspace or company joining one project |
| **Organization with shared projects** | Enterprise | Large companies with several workspaces (by region or business unit) that need to work on the same projects |

## Guest access

1. Open the project and click **Share > Invite**, then enter the email address.
2. A person who isn't a member of your workspace becomes a **Guest** and can open only the projects shared with them.
3. Guests switch between their own workspace and yours with the workspace picker.
4. Team and Business include 5 free guests per paid seat.

Limitations: guests don't see your member directory, can't create projects, and see their tasks from your workspace separately from their own.

## Enterprise Organizations

An **Organization** groups several Enterprise workspaces under one admin console.

1. An Organization Admin turns on **Org settings > Collaboration > Shared projects**.
2. In any workspace, a project manager clicks **Project > ⋯ > Share with workspaces** and chooses the target workspaces.
3. Members of those workspaces see the project in their own sidebar with their normal role, without becoming guests.
4. Reports and dashboards can include shared projects. Automations run in the workspace that **owns** the project.

## Limitations

- Shared projects can't be moved between workspaces. Ownership stays with the original workspace.
- Custom fields only from the owning workspace appear in a shared project.
- To combine two workspaces permanently, ask Support for a **workspace merge** (Enterprise, planned with the CSM).
