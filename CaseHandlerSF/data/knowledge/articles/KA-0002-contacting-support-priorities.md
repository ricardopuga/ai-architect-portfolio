## Support channels

| Plan | Channels | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Help center, community forum, email | Business hours, best effort |
| Team | Email, web form | Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00 ET |
| Business | Email, web form, chat | 24×5 |
| Enterprise | All above plus phone and named CSM | 24×7 for Urgent |

## Priority definitions

- **Urgent**: Production is down or unusable for most users, or payments/data integrity are affected, with no workaround. Examples: the mobile app crashes on launch for all users, the payment gateway is failing, invitation emails stop going out to everyone.
- **High**: A major feature is broken or badly degraded and the workaround is painful. Examples: webhooks returning 500, exports coming out empty, sensitive data appearing in exports.
- **Medium**: A feature misbehaves but a reasonable workaround exists, or the customer has a how-to question that blocks them.
- **Low**: General questions, feature requests, cosmetic issues.

Agents may change the priority the customer picked, and must add a case comment explaining why.

## First-response targets (Business and Enterprise)

| Priority | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent | 2 hours | 30 minutes |
| High | 4 hours | 2 hours |
| Medium | 1 business day | 8 hours |
| Low | 2 business days | 1 business day |

## Before you escalate

Attach the workspace ID, the affected user's email address, timestamps with timezone, and a HAR file for browser problems (see KA-0003).
